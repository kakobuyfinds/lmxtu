02 / Fit before favorites

iTaoBuy size guide:
measure the right thing.

A familiar size label is only a starting point. Keep the unit and measuring method alongside every number.

Comparison notes to record before choosing a size
Category Record these details Ask before deciding
Shoes Model, size system, foot-length guidance and width notes. Is the measurement foot length, insole length or outsole length?
Hoodies & jackets Flat chest width, shoulder width, body length and sleeve length. Was the garment measured flat, and how much room do you need for layering?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, thigh width and stretch information. Is the waist shown relaxed, stretched, flat or as a circumference?
Headwear Head circumference range, crown depth and closure style. Does the adjustment range include your measurement?
Jewelry Inner circumference, opening, width and stated materials. Does the listed dimension describe the inside fit or the outside shape?

03 / A usable research sheet

iTaoBuy comparison checklist

Give every product its own row. Keep different colors or versions separate when their photos, measurements or prices differ. A missing detail should stay marked “unverified.”

  • Identity Product name, category, model reference and exact color.
  • Source Product URL, seller identity and date you checked it.
  • Fit Selected size, measurements, units and measurement method.
  • Cost Item price, currency and separately confirmed extra charges.
  • Evidence Photo notes, materials stated by the seller and unanswered questions.
  • Decision Compare, ask for details, keep or remove—with a short reason.

04 / From browsing to a decision

iTaoBuy shopping guide:
a calmer way to compare.

Choose a small group of candidates and compare the same fields across all of them.

STEP / 01

Define the role.

Decide whether you need a daily sneaker, a warm layer or a finishing accessory. Set a fit preference and a spending limit before collecting links.

STEP / 02

Match the evidence.

Compare like with like: the same model, equivalent measurements and the exact variant. Similar thumbnails can hide different details.

STEP / 03

Resolve the gaps.

Revisit the destination listing for current information. Ask about missing dimensions or conditions before treating an item as ready to buy.

COMPARE THE TOTAL

Item cost + delivery + any applicable fees or taxes. Use one currency and a clearly recorded exchange rate when comparing listings.

06 / Straight answers

Before you
follow a find.

Clear definitions, practical checks and the limits of what this guide can tell you.

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

An iTaoBuy spreadsheet is a way to organize product research in rows, with fields for links, variants, measurements, price and review notes. This page is an independent comparison guide, not an official iTaoBuy spreadsheet or live inventory feed.

How do I compare two product links?

Compare the exact model, color, measurements, stated materials, included items and total cost. Record missing information as unverified rather than assuming two similar photos describe the same product.

Can I use one size conversion for every item?

No. Compare each listing's measurements and measurement method with an item you already own. Shoe length, garment dimensions, cap circumference and bracelet circumference need different checks.

Does this page show live stock or prices?

No. The brand images are visual references. Open the relevant CAFANS category and check the destination listing for current availability, price, seller details and purchase terms.

Where do the category links go?

The ten category links open the specified CAFANS category pages. General browsing links open https://www.cafans.com. This guide does not process orders or payments.
